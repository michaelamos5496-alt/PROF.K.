About

Commercial precision.
Documentary depth.

Chief Creative Officer & Managing Partner, OneShot Studios

Kwesi Apenteng-Mensah is a Ghanaian film director, creative director, and Chief Creative Officer & Managing Partner at OneShot Studios.

For more than a decade, he has led creative teams and directed films for brands and organisations including Uber, The World Bank, MTN Ghana, Unilever, Absa Ghana, Hubtel, Danone, Ashesi University, Africa Health Collaborative, KGL Group, DKT International, Cocoa360 and DStv Ghana.

His commercial work brings together strong visual ideas, considered performances and a clear understanding of the brand’s objective. His documentary practice centres on thoughtful interviews, careful observation and the responsibility of representing people’s experiences. Across both, he looks for the human truth that gives a story its power.

Kwesi collaborates with agencies, brand teams and organisations from treatment through final delivery, establishing a clear vision, guiding multidisciplinary teams and protecting the story through every production decision.

Alongside his client work, he helps develop original shows and formats through tv57, creating space for African voices, personalities and experiences.

Based in Accra, Ghana.

Approach

Understand what matters.
Find the story. Protect it.

Every project starts with the same questions: What must this film achieve? Who needs to care? What will make them believe it?

Those answers guide the treatment, the performances, the camera and the edit. My role is to keep the work purposeful while creating the conditions for something memorable to happen.
